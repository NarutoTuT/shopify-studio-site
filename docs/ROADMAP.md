# WhaleLeap Studio Roadmap

This roadmap tracks the website direction, not day-to-day commits.

## Current Direction

WhaleLeap Studio is being positioned as:

```text
Shopify Growth Engineering Studio for Chinese-founded global ecommerce brands.
```

The website should help visitors understand that WhaleLeap does more than build Shopify pages. The core offer is improving the Shopify foundation behind ecommerce growth:

- Engineering
- Conversion
- Tracking
- Performance

## P0: Core Commercial System

- Keep the homepage aligned with the Shopify Growth Engineering Studio positioning.
- Keep `/diagnosis` as the primary lead entry: Free Shopify Review.
- Complete the three core service pages:
  - Shopify Engineering
  - Conversion Optimization
  - Growth Analytics & Tracking
- Keep all primary CTAs pointed to `/diagnosis`.
- Make sure SEO pages and service pages do not compete for the same search intent.

## P1: SEO And Trust Building

- Add Chinese SEO content for:
  - Shopify 建站
  - Shopify 独立站
  - Shopify 转化优化
  - Shopify 主题开发
  - Shopify GA4 / GTM 数据追踪
- Add credible case studies when real project material is ready.
- Replace placeholder visuals with real Shopify project screenshots or approved partial UI captures.
- Improve About page trust signals:
  - founder background
  - Shopify experience
  - cross-border ecommerce experience
  - technical working style

## P2: Lead Operations

- Connect form leads to a lightweight CRM or database when lead volume increases.
- Add source tracking for SEO, GEO, social, referral, and paid traffic.
- Create a repeatable Free Shopify Review response template.
- Add a private lead review workflow if manual email handling becomes slow.

## P3: International Expansion

- Improve the English version after Chinese-facing positioning is stable.
- Decide whether the site needs language-specific URLs.
- Expand from overseas Chinese brands into broader US / EU DTC positioning when there are enough cases and proof.

## Current Priority

Finish the remaining service pages before adding new features:

```text
1. Conversion Optimization
2. Growth Analytics & Tracking
3. Theme Customization as an SEO/supporting page
```
