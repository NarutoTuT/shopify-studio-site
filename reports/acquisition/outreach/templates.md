# WhaleLeap Outreach Templates

Every final outreach message must be generated from a real lightweight audit.

Do not send fixed agency introductions. Do not use:

> Hi, we are a professional Shopify agency...

Variables:

- `{{brand}}`
- `{{main_observation}}`
- `{{why_it_matters}}`
- `{{free_review_offer}}`

## English Outreach

Hi {{brand}} team,

I was reviewing your Shopify storefront and noticed one specific issue that may be worth checking: {{main_observation}}.

{{why_it_matters}}

I am not sure if this is already on your roadmap, but {{free_review_offer}}.

No pitch deck. Just a short practical review you can use to decide whether it is worth fixing.

Best,
WhaleLeap Studio

## Human Approval Gate

No outreach should be sent automatically.

Before manual sending, confirm:

- The brand is real and publicly operating.
- Shopify fit has been verified or marked `null`.
- The main observation is based on public evidence.
- The message references only one main problem.
- No private personal data, guessed metrics, or fabricated contact details are included.
- The CTA is a Free Shopify Review, not an immediate discovery call.
