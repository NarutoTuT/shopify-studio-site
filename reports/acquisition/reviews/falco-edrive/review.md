# WhaleLeap Shopify Review

Brand: Falco eDrive

Website: https://falcoedrive.com/products/twm

Review Date: 2026-09-13

## Executive Summary

The TWM product page has a rich media story, but the mobile buying path makes purchase-critical information harder to reach. The first priority should be improving the mobile PDP hierarchy while keeping the media assets available.

## Priority 1 — Main Money Problem

Observed Fact

On the TWM mobile PDP, the page content begins with a long sequence of media controls before shoppers reach core buying details. The 390x844 mobile check also reported horizontal overflow.

Evidence

- URL: https://falcoedrive.com/products/twm
- Viewport: Mobile 390x844
- Screenshot: `reports/acquisition/reviews/falco-edrive/evidence/01-main-problem.webp`

Why It Matters

For a high-ticket mobility product, buyers need fast access to price, lead time, specs, and add-to-cart controls. A long media-first path may delay the information needed to continue purchase consideration.

Recommended Direction

Rebalance the mobile PDP so the title, price, lead time, key specs, and purchase controls appear earlier, while moving deeper media exploration below or into a cleaner gallery pattern.

Confidence: High

## Priority 2

Observed Fact

A signup popup appeared over the mobile PDP during review and asked for first name, email, and phone before the shopper could continue.

Evidence

- URL: https://falcoedrive.com/products/twm
- Viewport: Mobile 390x844
- Screenshot: `reports/acquisition/reviews/falco-edrive/evidence/01-main-problem.webp`

Why It Matters

An early modal can interrupt high-intent product evaluation, especially when the shopper has not yet reviewed price, lead time, or specs.

Recommended Direction

Review modal timing and targeting. Consider delaying it, suppressing it on product-entry sessions, or replacing it with a less intrusive capture point after product evaluation.

Confidence: Medium

## Priority 3

Observed Fact

The public page title references `Current Lead Time 6 Weeks`, while visible PDP text also references `Lead Time 3 to 6 Weeks`.

Evidence

- URL: https://falcoedrive.com/products/twm
- Viewport: Desktop 1440x900 and mobile 390x844
- Screenshot: `reports/acquisition/reviews/falco-edrive/evidence/02-secondary.webp`

Why It Matters

Lead time clarity matters for expensive mobility products. If shoppers see different lead-time wording, they may hesitate before purchase or need to contact support for confirmation.

Recommended Direction

Make lead-time messaging consistent across product title, PDP heading, badges, and purchase-support copy.

Confidence: Medium

## Recommended Order

1. Mobile PDP purchase-information hierarchy and overflow check
2. Popup timing on product-entry sessions
3. Lead-time message consistency

This order starts with the mobile purchase path, then removes interruption risk, then tightens decision-support messaging.

I did not access Shopify Admin, GA4, GTM, Google Ads, Meta Ads, checkout analytics, private sales data, or internal conversion reports. Any tracking, conversion, or revenue impact should be treated as a hypothesis until verified with internal data.
